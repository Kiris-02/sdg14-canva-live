Target 14.1: Eliminate Marine Pollution

Halting Land-Based Agricultural Runoff and Plastic Influx by 2025

✥ Kéo
ORIGIN POINT
80% Land-Based
Massive municipal waste, toxic chemicals, and fertilizers flowing down river basins.
✥ Kéo
GLOBAL MANDATE
Target 2025
Official UN deadline to prevent and significantly reduce marine pollution of all kinds.
✥ Kéo
PRIMARY VECTORS
N, P & Plastics
Excess reactive nitrogen, phosphorus, and macro to microplastics choking marine life.

✥ Kéo

✥ Kéo
SUB-INDICATOR 14.1.1(b)

✥ Kéo
ECOLOGICAL IMPACT
500+ Dead Zones
Hypoxic ocean dead zones worldwide stripped of dissolved oxygen by nutrient-fed algal blooms.

TARGET 14.3 QUESTION 1 • TARGET AIM Speaker 5

Target 14.3: Minimize Ocean Acidification

Confronting the Severe Chemical Cost of Planetary Carbon Absorption

✥ Kéo
PLANETARY SINK
25% CO2 Absorbed
Oceans absorb a quarter of anthropogenic carbon emissions, creating carbonic acid (H2CO3).
✥ Kéo
ACIDITY SURGE
+30% Acidity
A 0.1 unit drop in global surface seawater pH represents a 30% surge in hydrogen ion (H+) concentration.
✥ Kéo
CALCIFIER CRISIS
Corrosive Waters
Depletion of carbonate ions (CO3²⁻) dissolves shells of pteropods, shellfish, and coral skeletons.

✥ Kéo

✥ Kéo
CARBON SYSTEM PARTNERS

Reporting Total Dissolved Inorganic Carbon and Total Alkalinity alongside pH for full thermodynamic equations.
✥ Kéo
SATURATION HORIZON
Ω Aragonite
Tracking shoaling aragonite saturation depth where deep ocean currents become corrosive to bone.
